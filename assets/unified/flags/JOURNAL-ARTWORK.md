# Journal banner sources

These banners incorporate the journals’ actual marks for identification. The journal artwork retains its respective rights; it is not original site artwork or relicensed under GPL.

## Théorie Communiste

- Banner: `communization-theorie-communiste.svg`
- Original vector masthead: https://theoriecommuniste.org/squelettes/logo.svg
- Journal: https://theoriecommuniste.org/la-revue/
- Treatment: the original scarlet masthead and rules, centered on a white 3:2 field. Geometry and color preserved.

## Chuang

- Banner: `communization-chuang-journal.svg`
- Original journal mark: https://chuangcn.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/chuang1FRONT.png
- Journal: https://chuangcn.org/
- Treatment: the original, unmodified transparent PNG embedded at its native dimensions, centered on a black 3:2 field. No invented horse illustration.

Both sources were retrieved on 2026-10-08. The public thumbnail and display versions are generated from these source banners.
