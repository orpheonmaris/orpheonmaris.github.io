    About the sorter

    One tree, many traditions

    How does it work?

    Each answer narrows the remaining possibilities. Every completed quiz ends at one result, and each result has one complete route through the tree. You can inspect that route on the result card.

    How are distinctions handled?

    Spelling variants are grouped under one result. Political traditions can overlap, and some results represent a particular author, organization or period. A route shows this game’s distinctions; it does not establish that every adherent would answer the same way.

    What exactly is here?

    201 distinct outcomes, 184 reachable questions and 201 complete routes. Every result has artwork, a quotation or description, a family, a property branch and an inspectable answer path.

    Important limitation

    This is an exploratory classification game, not a validated psychological or political-science measure. Its labels and distinctions necessarily simplify contested traditions; inclusion does not imply endorsement.
